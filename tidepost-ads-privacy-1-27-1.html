Tidepost privacy policy for Android menu advertising

This policy covers Tidepost 1.27.1 for Android. For the previous release, see the Android 1.27.0 privacy policy. For the offline editions, see the offline privacy policy.

Your garden, deliveries and focus sessions stay on your device.

The coast, games, timer and music work offline. Tidepost 1.27.1 for Android offers an online banner at the bottom of the expanded coast menu. The iOS app and browser preview have no ads.

What stays on your device

Your settings, garden arrangements, delivery progress, selected harbor and focus timer details are saved locally so they are there when you return.

Tidepost has no user accounts and does not send this saved information to a server.

Your menu advertising choice on Android

The coast menu offers an optional age question. Your exact age is discarded. Skipping the question keeps the visit ad-free. An answer under 18 saves a restriction on this device that keeps ads off. All activities remain available.

For adults, the menu banner is on by default. Before requesting an ad, Tidepost contacts Google to check which privacy choices apply in your region and shows a consent form when required. Adults can turn the banner off in Menu advertising. Tidepost saves that preference locally. Ad privacy options are available there when required.

On a personal device, you can choose to remember your age group so you can skip the question on future visits. This saves only the adult age group on your device. Without this choice, adult status lasts for the current visit. Choose Switch user before sharing the device to clear adult status.

How the banner uses data

Google AdMob uses your IP address, which can indicate your approximate location. Its advertising services can also receive device or account identifiers, app and ad interactions such as taps, and diagnostic information such as crashes and load times. Google uses this information to deliver and measure advertising, understand performance and prevent fraud. The SDK encrypts information it sends.

Tidepost requests non-personalized ads. It does not send your entered age, garden, delivery records or timer details to Google. The harbor coordinates come from a built-in list. Tidepost does not request access to your device’s location.

Optional timer reminders

If you enable timer reminders, your device uses its notification, alarm and vibration features to alert you. Permission requests and reminder settings are optional; you can use the coast without enabling them.

Your device may show a timer notification, finish time or remaining time on its lock screen. You control notification visibility, sound and vibration in Tidepost and your phone’s settings.

Keeping or removing your data

Saved app data stays on your device until you remove it. On Android, clearing Tidepost’s app data in your phone’s settings erases its local settings and progress. Uninstalling Tidepost removes its local app data.

Clearing app storage also removes the local age restriction and stored privacy choices. Removing local data does not delete information Google already received. Google explains its use of advertising data and your choices at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. Your device or operating system controls any system backups.
