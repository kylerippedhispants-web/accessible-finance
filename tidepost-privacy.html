Tidepost Privacy Policy

Your little coast stays on your device.

Tidepost works offline. The app itself does not send information to the developer.

What stays on your device

Your settings, garden arrangements, delivery progress, selected harbor and focus timer details are saved locally so they are there when you return.

Tidepost has no user accounts and does not send this saved information to a server.

No tracking or advertising

Tidepost does not include advertising, analytics or tracking tools. The app does not make network requests or collect or share personal data with the developer or third parties.

The harbor names and coordinates are built into the app. Tidepost does not access your device’s location.

Optional timer reminders

If you enable timer reminders, your device uses its notification, alarm and vibration features to alert you. Permission requests and reminder settings are optional; you can use the coast without enabling them.

Your device may show a timer notification, finish time or remaining time on its lock screen. You control notification visibility, sound and vibration in Tidepost and your phone’s settings.

Keeping or removing your data

Saved app data stays on your device until you remove it. On Android, clearing Tidepost’s app data in your phone’s settings erases its local settings and progress. Uninstalling Tidepost removes its local app data.

There is no developer account or server copy to delete. Any backups managed by your device or operating system are controlled through that system’s settings.
