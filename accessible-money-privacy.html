Accessible Money Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 25, 2026

Accessible Money is a personal finance tracking and educational planning app from Accessible Finance. This policy describes the Android app, package ca.accessiblefinance.mobile. The Accessible Finance website and its optional cloud Planner are separate services covered by the website privacy policy.

Your information stays on your device during ordinary use

The app stores the information you enter on your device and uses it to display your records and calculate budgets, reports, net worth, goals, and illustrative FIRE targets. It does not connect to your bank, create an online account, automatically synchronize with the website Planner, or upload your financial records to Accessible Finance.

Local records can include your optional profile name, currency and region preferences, account names and balances, transactions and notes, categories, budgets, savings goals, categorization rules, net-worth history, and FIRE planning inputs. An older profile or an imported backup may also contain an email address. This is a local profile field, not a requirement to sign up for an online service.

App lock and security

The app encrypts its financial database on the device. The database key and local app-lock credentials are stored using the operating system's secure storage. You may choose an app PIN and, on supported devices, biometric unlock. Biometric authentication is handled by the operating system; Accessible Money does not receive or store your fingerprint or facial biometric template.

Android automatic app backup is disabled. Keep your device lock enabled and protect any backup passwords. Accessible Finance cannot recover a forgotten backup password or remotely restore financial records that you have not backed up.

Backups, imports, and sharing you choose

You can create a password-encrypted backup and choose where to save or share it. A backup includes financial records and profile metadata, but excludes your app PIN and the device's database encryption key. Importing a backup requires you to choose a file and provide its password.

You can also choose to share a financial report after a confirmation in the app. A report contains financial totals and is not encrypted by the app. Review the destination before sharing it. Your chosen file-storage provider, messaging service, or recipient controls the copy it receives under its own terms. If you choose a cloud-backed folder or another app, that service may upload or retain the file. Accessible Finance does not automatically receive these exports.

Advertising and usage measurement

Advertising is disabled in this release. The app does not request or display ads. Accessible Finance has not enabled an app analytics or crash-reporting service in this release. The device operating system and Google Play may process installation, security, or diagnostic information under their own settings and privacy terms.

Permissions and external links

Optional biometric unlock uses the device's authentication capability. File selection and sharing use system interfaces so you can choose a backup or its destination. The app does not need your contacts, precise location, microphone, or camera for its current features.

Choosing “Visit website” opens the Accessible Finance website in your browser. The app does not append your financial records or profile to that link. The website and any other service you visit may process information described in their own privacy policies.

Retention and deletion

Your local information remains until you change or remove it. You can edit or delete individual financial records. “Start fresh” in the app's data management screen replaces your financial records with an empty set; your local profile and app lock remain.

To remove the app's local profile, settings, lock credentials, and financial database on Android, clear the app's storage in Android settings or uninstall the app. Backups and reports you saved outside the app remain wherever you saved or shared them until you delete those copies or ask their recipient or provider to remove them.

Accessible Finance does not hold an online copy of your app's local financial database, so it cannot access, correct, recover, or remotely delete that local information for you.

Contact and policy changes

For privacy questions about Accessible Money, email accessiblefinanceca@gmail.com and identify the app in your message. Contact messages are handled by the service you choose. Do not send account numbers, passwords, backup files, or sensitive financial documents.

If a future release changes how the app handles information, this policy will be updated to describe that release. Check the policy date and release details when reviewing changes.
