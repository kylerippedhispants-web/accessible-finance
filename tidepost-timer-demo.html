A little time for yourself.

Tidepost’s optional focus timer keeps a visible countdown while you step away from the coast. This recording shows how its Android notification starts, pauses and finishes.

Follow the timer

A one-minute session is started, shown in the notification shade and on the lock screen, then paused and resumed. At its real deadline, the completion reminder replaces the countdown. Select a moment below to seek in the recording.

About this recording

Recorded on a dedicated test emulator on October 18, 2026. The original recording is unedited, with no accelerated timer or elapsed time. Notification and alarm permissions were configured on the test device before recording.

This screen recording has no audio. It demonstrates notification behavior; it does not demonstrate the alarm’s sound or physical vibration.
