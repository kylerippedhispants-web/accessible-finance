Read the map

Three useful signals in one picture

A cash-flow map is not a judgment. It is a snapshot that helps you decide what deserves attention first.

01

Band width shows priority

The widest expense bands are where small percentage changes can have the largest effect. Start there before trimming many tiny categories.

02

Saving is a planned outflow

Treating saving and investing as their own category makes the future visible instead of hoping something remains at the end of the period.

03

A shortfall needs a funding source

When expenses exceed income, the map adds a shortfall band. In real life, that gap is usually covered by cash reserves, credit, or reduced spending.
